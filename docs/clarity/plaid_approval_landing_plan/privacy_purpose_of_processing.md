# Clarity Privacy Purpose Of Processing

Status: File 04 Phase 4 purpose-of-processing section approved for initial landing launch draft.

Purpose: explain why Clarity uses each category of information in plain language, with clear limits around selling data, unrelated advertising, regulated advice, and unsupported automation.

## Draft Section Title

Preferred:

- `How Clarity uses information`

Acceptable alternatives:

- `Why Clarity uses data`
- `How information supports the product`

Avoid:

- `How we exploit data`
- `How we monetize data`
- `How we use everything`

## Plain-Language Intro

Recommended draft:

> Clarity uses information to provide the product, keep accounts and financial context organized, support Rex, improve reliability, respond to requests, protect the service, and meet legal or compliance obligations.

Required boundary:

> Clarity does not sell personal financial data.

Optional boundary:

> Clarity does not use personal financial data for unrelated third-party advertising.

## Product Operation

Clarity may use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Authenticate users.
- Maintain app settings and preferences.
- Sync user data across supported devices.
- Provide the mobile app and public website.
- Route requests to the backend services needed to operate Clarity.

Plain-language copy:

> We use account and app information to run Clarity, sign you in, keep your settings, and provide the features you choose to use.

## Financial Organization And Insights

Clarity may use financial account and transaction information to:

- Organize transactions.
- Categorize spending.
- Display dashboard summaries.
- Compare income and spending.
- Calculate budget progress.
- Show spending pressure or patterns.
- Connect transactions to accounts and categories.
- Support duplicate detection and import troubleshooting.

Plain-language copy:

> We use authorized financial context to organize spending, support budgets, and show summaries that help you review your money.

Boundaries:

- Do not claim exact forecasting.
- Do not claim guaranteed savings.
- Do not imply Clarity is financial advice.
- Do not imply real-time data if data timing depends on provider or institution support.

## Rex Assistant And Personalization

Clarity may use chat, voice, memory/context, goals, and financial context to:

- Generate Rex responses.
- Maintain conversation history.
- Answer questions about spending, budgets, goals, and saved context.
- Suggest or create memory candidates where supported.
- Apply user-approved memory or context.
- Provide more relevant responses over time.

Plain-language copy:

> Rex may use Clarity context, such as conversations, approved memory, budgets, goals, and authorized financial information, to answer questions and make conversations more useful.

Boundaries:

- Rex is not a financial advisor.
- Rex does not connect directly to banks outside Clarity's account connection flow.
- Voice features should be described as user-initiated, not continuously listening.

## Voice Features

Clarity may use voice/audio-derived information to:

- Convert user speech to text.
- Send transcript/context to Rex.
- Generate text responses.
- Create spoken responses through text-to-speech.
- Diagnose and improve voice reliability.

Plain-language copy:

> When you use voice features, Clarity may process audio or transcripts so Rex can understand and respond.

Boundary:

- Do not say voice audio is never processed by vendors unless that is verified.

## Support, Contact, Waitlist, And Deletion Requests

Clarity may use contact/support information to:

- Respond to questions.
- Manage beta or waitlist requests.
- Process privacy, deletion, or security requests.
- Troubleshoot account or product issues.
- Keep records of support interactions where needed.

Plain-language copy:

> We use contact and support information to respond to requests, manage access, and handle privacy, deletion, or security questions.

Boundary:

- Public support forms should not request bank credentials or highly sensitive identifiers.

## Security, Fraud Prevention, And Service Integrity

Clarity may use logs, device/app information, authentication metadata, and service metadata to:

- Protect accounts.
- Detect errors, abuse, or unauthorized activity.
- Debug service issues.
- Maintain backend readiness and reliability.
- Monitor performance.
- Enforce terms and product boundaries.

Plain-language copy:

> We use technical and log information to keep Clarity working, diagnose issues, and help protect the service.

Boundary:

- Avoid promising specific security outcomes or certifications unless verified.

## Analytics And Product Improvement

Clarity may use limited product or website information to:

- Understand which features are used.
- Find broken flows.
- Improve reliability and usability.
- Evaluate high-level product performance.

Plain-language copy:

> We may use limited product and website information to understand reliability, fix problems, and improve Clarity.

Boundaries:

- Do not imply unrelated third-party ad targeting.
- Do not call data anonymous unless truly anonymized.
- If analytics vendors are added, disclose them in vendor/sharing sections.

## Legal, Compliance, And Safety

Clarity may use information to:

- Comply with applicable law.
- Respond to lawful requests.
- Enforce Terms of Service.
- Protect users, Clarity, or others from harm, fraud, abuse, or security threats.
- Support Plaid, app store, vendor, or other compliance reviews where relevant.

Plain-language copy:

> We may use information when needed to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect the service, or complete required compliance and security reviews.

## No-Sale And Advertising Boundaries

Required copy:

> Clarity does not sell personal financial data.

Recommended copy:

> Clarity does not use personal financial data for unrelated third-party advertising.

Do not say:

- `Clarity never shares data` because vendors/processors are used.
- `Clarity never uses analytics` unless analytics are not used and will not be added.
- `Clarity never processes voice data` because voice features require processing.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Uses include app operation, personalization, transaction categorization, budgeting, Rex, voice, support, security, analytics/product improvement, and compliance.
- States Clarity does not sell personal financial data.
- Does not imply unrelated ad targeting.
- Pairs general product-improvement language with concrete examples.
- Avoids financial advice, guaranteed outcome, exact forecasting, and unsupported security claims.
