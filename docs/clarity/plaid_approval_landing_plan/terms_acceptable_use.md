# Clarity Terms Acceptable Use

Status: File 05 Phase 6 acceptable use section approved for initial landing launch draft.

## Purpose

This contract defines user-readable acceptable-use language for Clarity's Terms of Service. It should describe prohibited behavior clearly without becoming overly broad, hostile, or hard to understand.

The section should protect Clarity, users, financial data, Rex, infrastructure, and third-party providers.

## Draft Section Titles

Preferred:

- `Acceptable use`

Acceptable alternatives:

- `Rules for using Clarity`
- `Things you may not do`
- `Using Clarity responsibly`

Avoid:

- `User misconduct`
- `Prohibited exploitation`
- `Unauthorized abuse covenant`

## Plain-Language Intro

Recommended draft:

> You agree to use Clarity responsibly and only for lawful purposes. You may not misuse Clarity, interfere with the service, access data without permission, or use Rex or Clarity to harm others.

This sets the tone before the examples.

## Account And Access Misuse

Users may not:

- Access or try to access another user's account, data, financial context, conversations, memory, or settings.
- Use another person's account without permission.
- Share credentials in a way that bypasses access controls.
- Misrepresent identity, authority, affiliation, or account ownership.
- Connect financial accounts or upload data that they are not authorized to use.
- Attempt to bypass authentication, row-level security, permissions, rate limits, or access controls.

## Technical Misuse

Users may not:

- Reverse engineer, decompile, scrape, crawl, copy, or extract Clarity systems except where allowed by law.
- Probe, scan, test, or attack Clarity systems without permission.
- Interfere with service availability, infrastructure, APIs, databases, or third-party providers.
- Introduce malware, harmful code, spam, automated abuse, credential stuffing, or denial-of-service activity.
- Abuse bugs, undocumented endpoints, or internal features.

Legal review note:

- Confirm reverse-engineering wording before launch, because some jurisdictions limit how this can be stated.

## Harmful Or Illegal Use

Users may not use Clarity to:

- Break the law.
- Violate another person's rights.
- Commit fraud, theft, harassment, abuse, or discrimination.
- Generate or submit harmful, deceptive, abusive, exploitative, or unlawful content.
- Infringe intellectual property or privacy rights.
- Upload content they do not have permission to use.

Keep examples concise and avoid turning the Terms into a policy encyclopedia.

## Financial And Sensitive Data Misuse

Users may not:

- Submit bank passwords, full account numbers, Social Security numbers, full card numbers, or similarly sensitive credentials through public contact forms or support email.
- Connect accounts they do not own or are not authorized to connect.
- Use Clarity to impersonate a financial institution, service provider, or another person.
- Use Clarity to facilitate financial fraud, unauthorized account access, or deceptive financial activity.

This aligns with Plaid review and the Privacy/Security pages.

## Rex And AI Misuse

Users may not use Rex or Clarity to:

- Generate instructions for fraud, account takeover, phishing, credential theft, or evading security.
- Make or assist decisions for another person without authorization.
- Misrepresent AI-generated content as professional financial, legal, tax, medical, or other licensed advice.
- Create harmful, deceptive, harassing, or abusive content.
- Attempt to extract system prompts, hidden instructions, private keys, credentials, internal logs, or other protected information.
- Bypass safety controls, rate limits, memory review controls, account boundaries, or data-access restrictions.

Tone note:

- The public Terms should frame this as responsible use, not as an accusation that users will misuse Rex.

## Enforcement And Response

Recommended draft:

> If we believe someone is misusing Clarity or creating risk for users, the service, or third-party providers, we may investigate, limit access, suspend or terminate accounts, remove content where appropriate, or take other reasonable steps to protect Clarity and users.

Legal review note:

- Confirm enforcement wording together with Phase 7 limitation/changes language.

## Wording Rules

Use:

- `use Clarity responsibly`
- `lawful purposes`
- `without permission`
- `protect Clarity and users`
- `reasonable steps`
- `where appropriate`

Avoid:

- `we can do anything at any time`
- `you waive all rights`
- `we monitor everything`
- `all violations are crimes`
- Overly aggressive legal language that makes the product feel hostile

## Acceptance Checklist

- Prohibits misuse, unauthorized access, reverse engineering, scraping, abuse, illegal activity, and harmful content.
- Includes AI/Rex misuse boundaries.
- Includes financial and sensitive-data misuse boundaries.
- Keeps language user-readable and not excessively broad.
- Includes a reasonable enforcement response draft.
- Flags reverse-engineering and enforcement language for legal review.
