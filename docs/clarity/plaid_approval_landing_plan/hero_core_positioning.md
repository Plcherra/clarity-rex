# Clarity Hero Core Positioning

Status: File 02 Phase 1 core positioning approved for initial landing launch.

Purpose: define the first sentence users and Plaid reviewers should understand about Clarity before any deeper product, privacy, or security copy.

## Positioning Contract

Clarity is:

- A personal AI financial co-pilot.
- Built to help users organize transactions, budgets, spending context, and decisions.
- Supported by Rex, the AI assistant inside Clarity.
- Designed around user-authorized financial account connections and clear data controls.

Clarity is not:

- A bank.
- A broker.
- A lender.
- A tax advisor.
- An investment advisor.
- A product endorsed by Plaid.
- A service that guarantees financial outcomes.

## Primary Hero Promise

Approved core promise:

> Clarity is a personal AI financial co-pilot that helps you understand your spending, budgets, and money decisions with Rex by your side.

This sentence should be treated as the source of truth for early landing copy.

Co-pilot language means assistant support and user-controlled context. It must not imply Clarity or Rex makes financial decisions for the user.

## Headline Direction

Preferred headline:

> Your personal AI financial co-pilot.

Acceptable alternatives:

- `Understand your money with Clarity and Rex.`
- `A calmer way to understand spending, budgets, and decisions.`
- `Clarity helps you make sense of your money.`

Avoid headlines that:

- Promise to fix finances.
- Promise savings.
- Sound like regulated financial advice.
- Make Rex look like the product name.
- Overemphasize AI at the expense of financial context and trust.

## Supporting Copy Direction

Preferred supporting copy:

> Clarity brings transactions, budgets, goals, and context together so Rex can help you review patterns, understand tradeoffs, and make decisions with more context.

Required meaning:

- Clarity organizes financial context.
- Rex helps explain and reason about that context.
- The user remains in control.
- The product supports decisions rather than making decisions for the user.

## Rex Naming Rule

Approved Rex wording:

- `Rex, the AI assistant inside Clarity`
- `Rex helps you ask questions about your money`
- `Rex can help explain spending, budgets, goals, and saved context`

Forbidden Rex wording:

- `Rex is the product`
- `Sign up for Rex`
- `Connect your bank to Rex`
- `Rex manages your money`
- `Rex makes financial decisions for you`

## Financial Advice Boundary

Hero and value proposition copy must avoid:

- `financial advisor`
- `investment advice`
- `tax advice`
- `guaranteed savings`
- `fix your finances`
- `automatically optimizes your money`
- `manages your money for you`

Allowed safer language:

- `organize`
- `understand`
- `review`
- `support`
- `insights`
- `context`
- `tradeoffs`
- `decisions`

## Plaid Review Alignment

The hero does not need to explain all Plaid details. Those belong in the trust bar, consent section, FAQ, Privacy, and Security pages.

The hero must still avoid:

- Plaid endorsement claims.
- Bank partnership claims.
- Claims that financial account access happens without user permission.
- Claims that Clarity can provide guaranteed outcomes.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Headline says or clearly implies Clarity is a personal AI financial co-pilot.
- Rex is introduced as the assistant inside Clarity.
- Copy does not promise to fix finances.
- Copy does not imply Clarity is regulated financial advice.
- Copy supports both user trust and Plaid review clarity.
