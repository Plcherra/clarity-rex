## Services Covered

The Privacy Policy should cover:

- The Clarity mobile app.
- The public Clarity website.
- Rex, the AI assistant inside Clarity.
- Account connection and financial-data features.
- Budgeting, transaction organization, memory/context, goals, chat, and voice features.
- Support, waitlist, contact, and deletion request flows.

The policy should not cover:

- Third-party websites not operated by Clarity.
- Bank or financial institution privacy practices.
- Plaid's own privacy practices beyond explaining that Plaid may process data as a third-party provider.
- App stores, device operating systems, or unrelated third-party services.

## Audience

The policy must be understandable to:

- Clarity users.
- Plaid reviewers.
- Future beta/waitlist users.
- People submitting privacy, support, or deletion requests.

It should be written in plain language and avoid legal-only phrasing where a simple sentence works.

## Opening Scope Copy

Recommended draft:

> This Privacy Policy explains how Clarity handles information when you use the Clarity mobile app, public website, Rex assistant, account connection features, budgeting tools, transaction organization, chat, voice, memory/context features, support, waitlist, and related services.

Recommended follow-up:

> Clarity is designed to help users organize financial context, review spending and budgets, and ask Rex questions using user-authorized product data. Clarity is not a bank, broker, lender, tax advisor, investment advisor, or financial advisor.

## Required Plain-Language Boundaries

The scope section must make clear:

- Clarity is the product.
- Rex is the assistant inside Clarity.
- Account connection is user-authorized.
- Financial data is used to provide Clarity features.
- Public website visitors should not submit bank credentials, account numbers, or sensitive financial details through public contact forms.
- Legal/operator details require final confirmation before launch.
