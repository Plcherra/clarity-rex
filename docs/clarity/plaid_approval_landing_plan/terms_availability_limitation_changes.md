# Clarity Terms Availability Limitation And Changes

Status: File 05 Phase 7 availability, limitation, and changes section approved for initial landing launch draft.

## Purpose

This contract defines the Terms of Service language for service availability, product changes, limitation of liability, disclaimers, and Terms updates.

This is legal-sensitive language. It must remain clearly marked for attorney review before production launch or broad public use.

## Draft Section Titles

Preferred:

- `Service availability and changes`
- `Limitations`
- `Changes to these Terms`

Acceptable alternatives:

- `Availability`
- `Service changes`
- `Liability limits`

Avoid:

- `We are never responsible`
- `No liability`
- `You waive everything`
- `Terms can change whenever`

## Service Availability

Recommended draft:

> Clarity is provided using app, backend, AI, voice, account connection, hosting, database, and third-party services. The service may be unavailable, delayed, interrupted, inaccurate, or changed from time to time.

Plain-language examples:

- Maintenance.
- Bugs.
- Network or device issues.
- Third-party provider issues.
- Financial institution or Plaid availability.
- AI, speech-to-text, or text-to-speech provider issues.
- App store, operating-system, or device-permission behavior.

## Product Changes

Recommended draft:

> We may add, change, limit, suspend, or remove features as Clarity develops, including beta, trial, experimental, or early-access features.

Boundary:

- Do not promise all features will remain forever.
- Do not imply every beta feature is production-ready.
- Do not imply Plaid-connected features are guaranteed for every user or institution.

## Accuracy And No Warranty Draft

Recommended draft:

> Clarity is provided on an "as available" basis. We work to make Clarity useful and reliable, but we do not guarantee that the service, data, connected account information, categories, budgets, Rex responses, voice transcripts, or other outputs will always be accurate, complete, current, uninterrupted, secure, or error-free.

Legal review note:

- Confirm warranty disclaimer wording before public launch.
- Some jurisdictions limit how disclaimers can be presented.

## Limitation Of Liability Draft

Recommended draft:

> To the fullest extent allowed by law, Clarity and its operators will not be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost data, lost opportunities, or financial losses arising from use of or inability to use Clarity.

Optional follow-up for legal review:

> To the fullest extent allowed by law, Clarity's total liability for any claim relating to the service will be limited to the amount you paid to use Clarity during the period required by applicable law, or another amount approved through legal review.

Important:

- Do not publish a specific liability cap until legal review confirms it.
- If Clarity is free at launch, legal review should decide whether a nominal cap is appropriate.

## Third-Party Dependency Boundary

Recommended draft:

> Clarity may rely on third-party services, including account connection, hosting, database, AI, voice, email, analytics, app stores, and device platforms. We do not control third-party services and are not responsible for their separate terms, privacy practices, outages, data availability, or decisions.

This should align with Privacy, Security, and Plaid connection terms.

## Terms Update Process

Recommended draft:

> We may update these Terms as Clarity changes. If we make material changes, we will provide notice by updating the date on the Terms page, posting a notice, sending a message, or using another reasonable method. Continued use of Clarity after updated Terms become effective means you accept the updated Terms.

Launch note:

- Confirm whether account users receive in-app/email notice for material changes.
- Confirm whether the public site will show a last-updated date.

## Beta And Early Access Note

Recommended draft:

> Some features may be beta, experimental, or early access. These features may change, break, be removed, or produce unexpected results.

Use this only if public launch includes beta language or waitlist/trial access.

## Wording Rules

Use:

- `to the fullest extent allowed by law`
- `as available`
- `reasonable notice`
- `material changes`
- `may be interrupted`
- `may change`
- `legal review required`

Avoid:

- `we are never liable`
- `you have no remedies`
- `we can change anything without notice`
- `we guarantee uptime`
- `we guarantee security`
- `we guarantee accuracy`
- `all third-party failures are your problem`

## Legal Review Flags

Attorney review is required for:

- Warranty disclaimer language.
- Liability limitation language.
- Any specific liability cap.
- Arbitration, venue, governing law, or dispute-resolution clauses if added later.
- Age, jurisdiction, consumer-rights, or regional compliance language.
- Whether beta/early-access disclaimers are sufficient.

## Acceptance Checklist

- States service may change, be delayed, be interrupted, or be unavailable.
- Covers third-party dependency limits, including financial institutions, Plaid, AI, voice, hosting, app stores, and device platforms.
- Includes a reasonable limitation of liability draft with legal-review flags.
- Includes an "as available" / no-warranty draft with legal-review flags.
- Includes a Terms update process.
- Avoids absolute disclaimers, unsupported security/uptime/accuracy guarantees, and hostile wording.
