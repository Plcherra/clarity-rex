# Clarity Terms Scope

Status: File 05 Phase 1 terms scope approved for initial landing launch draft.

## Purpose

This contract defines the scope and identity requirements for Clarity's public Terms of Service. It should help the `/terms` page explain what the Terms cover without implying unsupported legal finality.

The Terms are product-specific launch copy and must be reviewed before production use, especially before broad public launch or Plaid production access.

- `/terms`

Page title:

- `Terms of Service`

Product name:

- `Clarity`

Assistant naming:

- `Rex` should be described as the AI assistant inside Clarity.

Effective date:

- `Effective date: [effective date]`

Last updated:

- `Last updated: [last updated date]`

Launch note:

- Replace date placeholders with the final publication date before deployment.

## Operator And Contact Placeholders

The final Terms must identify the operator of Clarity.

Current placeholder:

- `Operator: [Clarity operator/legal entity to be confirmed]`

Do not publish repository ownership, personal names, or internal testing identities as the legal operator until confirmed.

Required final fields:

- Legal/operator name.
- Support contact email or contact route.
- Privacy contact route.
- Security contact route if separate.
- Mailing address if required by legal review.

Recommended placeholder copy:

> These Terms are between you and [operator/legal entity], the operator of Clarity. For questions about Clarity or these Terms, contact us at [support/contact email].

## Services Covered

The Terms should cover:

- The Clarity mobile app.
- The public Clarity website.
- Rex, the AI assistant inside Clarity.
- Account connection and financial-data features.
- Budgeting, transaction organization, goals, memory/context, chat, and voice features.
- Waitlist, contact, support, and deletion request flows.
- Backend services, APIs, and related product infrastructure used to provide Clarity.

The Terms should not claim to control:

- Bank or financial institution services.
- Plaid's own services or privacy practices.
- App store terms, device operating systems, or third-party websites.
- Professional financial, tax, legal, accounting, banking, lending, brokerage, or investment services.

## Opening Scope Copy

Recommended draft:

> These Terms of Service explain the rules for using Clarity, including the Clarity mobile app, public website, Rex assistant, account connection features, budgeting tools, transaction organization, chat, voice, memory/context, support, waitlist, and related services.

Recommended follow-up:

> Clarity helps users organize financial context, review spending and budgets, and ask Rex questions using product data they choose to provide or connect. Clarity is not a bank, broker, lender, tax advisor, investment advisor, financial advisor, accountant, or law firm.

## Required Plain-Language Boundaries

The scope section must make clear:

- Clarity is the product.
- Rex is the assistant inside Clarity.
- Account connection is user-authorized.
- Users remain responsible for reviewing information and decisions.
- Clarity does not move money, open accounts, provide credit, or execute transactions for users.
- Third-party services may have separate terms and privacy practices.
- Legal/operator details require final confirmation before launch.

## Cross-Links Needed

The Terms should link to:

- Privacy Policy.
- Security and Data Handling.
- Data Deletion.
- Contact.

The footer and relevant onboarding/account flows should link back to the Terms once implemented.
