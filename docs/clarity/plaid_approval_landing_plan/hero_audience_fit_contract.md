# Clarity Hero Audience Fit Contract

Status: File 02 Phase 5 audience fit approved for initial landing launch.

Purpose: make the hero language work for personal finance users and Plaid reviewers at the same time: clear, calm, useful, and compliance-aware.

## Audience Contract

The hero must speak to two audiences:

1. Users who want help understanding personal finances.
2. Reviewers who need to understand the product purpose, data use, and compliance posture quickly.

The copy should feel useful to users and inspectable to reviewers.

## Tone Rules

Hero copy should be:

- Calm.
- Direct.
- Professional.
- Human.
- Specific to the product.
- Easy to understand on a phone.

Hero copy should not be:

- Hype-heavy.
- Fear-based.
- Guilt-driven.
- Slangy.
- Overly technical.
- Legalistic in the first sentence.
- Vague enough to describe any finance app.

## User-Facing Needs

Users should understand:

- Clarity helps organize money context.
- Rex can help explain spending, budgets, goals, and decisions.
- They stay in control.
- They can inspect data handling before requesting access.

Use examples like:

- Transactions.
- Budgets.
- Spending patterns.
- Goals.
- Decisions.

Avoid starting with:

- Vendor names.
- Database terms.
- API concepts.
- Compliance jargon.

## Plaid Reviewer Needs

Plaid reviewers should understand:

- Clarity has a clear personal finance use case.
- Financial data supports transaction organization, budgeting, insights, and Rex context.
- Account connection is user-authorized.
- Data handling, deletion, security, privacy, and terms are easy to reach.

The hero should not carry every detail, but it should make the use case obvious enough that the rest of the page has context.

## Approved Copy Shape

Recommended hero structure:

1. Short headline.
2. One supporting sentence.
3. Primary and secondary CTA.
4. Short trust subcopy.

Example structure:

> Your personal AI financial co-pilot.
>
> Clarity brings transactions, budgets, goals, and context together so Rex can help you review patterns, understand tradeoffs, and make decisions with more context.

Then CTA pair:

- `Request access`
- `See how data is handled`

Then trust subcopy:

> Connect accounts only when you choose, with clear paths to disconnect accounts or request deletion.

## Copy To Avoid

Avoid:

- `Take control of your financial future`
- `Fix your finances with AI`
- `Never worry about money again`
- `Rex manages your finances`
- `Automatically optimize your spending`
- `Bank-grade AI finance platform`
- `The Plaid-powered financial advisor`
- `Guaranteed smarter financial decisions`

## Specificity Checklist

Hero copy should include at least two of these concepts:

- Transactions.
- Budgets.
- Spending patterns.
- Goals.
- Money decisions.
- Rex.
- User-authorized account connection.

If none of those concepts appear, the copy is too generic.

## Readability Requirements

- Headline should be short enough to fit on mobile without awkward breaks.
- Supporting copy should be one or two sentences.
- Avoid dense commas and long clauses.
- Keep the first viewport scannable.
- Do not bury the product name below the hero visual.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Copy is calm, direct, and professional.
- Copy avoids slang, hype, guilt, and pressure tactics.
- Copy works for both users and Plaid reviewers.
- Copy mentions transaction review, budgets, Rex, or another specific Clarity surface.
- Copy does not imply regulated advice or guaranteed outcomes.
