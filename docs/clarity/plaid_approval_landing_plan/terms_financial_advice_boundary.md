# Clarity Terms Financial Advice Boundary

Status: File 05 Phase 3 financial advice boundary approved for initial landing launch draft.

## Purpose

This contract defines the Terms of Service language that explains what Clarity can help with and what it does not provide. The goal is to protect the product from regulated-advice positioning while keeping the copy calm, useful, and confidence-building.

Clarity should be positioned as organization, analysis, insight, and coaching support. It should not be positioned as a bank, broker, lender, investment advisor, tax advisor, accountant, law firm, or regulated financial advisor.

## Draft Section Titles

Preferred:

- `Financial information and decisions`

Acceptable alternatives:

- `No professional financial advice`
- `Your financial decisions`
- `Clarity is a financial organization tool`

Avoid:

- `Financial advice`
- `Investment recommendations`
- `Guaranteed financial results`
- `Professional services`

## Product Positioning

Recommended draft:

> Clarity helps you organize financial information, review spending, understand budgets, and ask Rex questions about context available inside the product. Clarity may provide summaries, explanations, patterns, educational information, and coaching-style support.

This should be paired with the boundary language below so the value proposition stays useful but not regulated.

## Required Boundary Copy

Recommended draft:

> Clarity is not a bank, broker, lender, tax advisor, accountant, investment advisor, financial advisor, law firm, or credit counseling service. Clarity does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, accounting, legal, lending, brokerage, or banking advice.

Recommended follow-up:

> You are responsible for reviewing information, deciding what actions to take, and consulting qualified professionals before making important financial, tax, legal, credit, or investment decisions.

## Rex-Specific Boundary

Recommended draft:

> Rex may help explain spending, budgets, goals, and product context, but Rex does not make decisions for you, execute transactions, move money, open accounts, apply for credit, file taxes, or provide professional advice.

This keeps Rex powerful as an assistant without making it sound autonomous or regulated.

## Examples Of Supported Help

Clarity may help users:

- Organize transactions and categories.
- Review income, spending, and budget progress.
- Surface spending patterns or pressure.
- Ask questions about product context.
- Understand their own goals and plans.
- Receive educational or coaching-style explanations.
- Prepare questions to ask a qualified professional.

## Examples Of Unsupported Claims

Do not say Clarity or Rex can:

- Guarantee savings.
- Guarantee budget success.
- Predict exact future balances.
- Recommend specific investments.
- Provide tax filing instructions as professional advice.
- Decide whether a user should take debt, credit, insurance, mortgage, or investment action.
- Replace a licensed professional.
- Make financial decisions automatically.
- Move money or execute transactions.

## Important-Decision Guidance

Recommended draft:

> For important decisions, such as taxes, investments, credit, insurance, loans, legal matters, or major financial commitments, you should consult a qualified professional who can consider your full situation.

This should appear in the Terms and may also appear in FAQ/legal footer copy if needed.

## Plaid-Friendly Notes

The financial advice boundary should reinforce:

- Plaid is used for user-authorized account connection.
- Connected data supports organization and analysis inside Clarity.
- Plaid does not provide Rex or AI advice features.
- Clarity does not claim Plaid endorses its financial insights.
- Clarity does not promise continuous, complete, or real-time data availability.

## Wording Rules

Use:

- `organize financial information`
- `review spending`
- `understand budgets`
- `summaries and explanations`
- `educational information`
- `coaching-style support`
- `you remain responsible for your decisions`
- `consult qualified professionals`

Avoid:

- `financial advisor`
- `investment advice`
- `tax advice`
- `guaranteed savings`
- `exact forecast`
- `automatic money management`
- `Rex decides`
- `Rex invests`
- `Rex pays`
- `Plaid-powered financial advisor`

## Acceptance Checklist

- States Clarity provides organization, analysis, educational information, and coaching-style support.
- States Clarity is not a bank, broker, lender, tax advisor, accountant, investment advisor, financial advisor, law firm, or credit counseling service.
- Encourages qualified professional advice for important decisions.
- Makes clear users remain responsible for decisions.
- States Rex does not move money, execute transactions, open accounts, apply for credit, file taxes, or make decisions for users.
- Avoids undermining Clarity's usefulness while keeping regulated-advice boundaries clear.
