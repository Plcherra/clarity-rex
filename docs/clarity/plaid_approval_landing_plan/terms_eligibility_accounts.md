# Clarity Terms Eligibility And Accounts

Status: File 05 Phase 2 eligibility and accounts section approved for initial landing launch draft.

## Purpose

This contract defines the Terms of Service language for who may use Clarity, what users are responsible for when creating or maintaining an account, and where account issues should be directed.

The section should be clear and user-readable. It should not become a dense legal clause.

## Draft Section Titles

Preferred:

- `Eligibility and accounts`

Acceptable alternatives:

- `Using your account`
- `Account responsibilities`

Avoid:

- `Legal capacity and representations`
- `User warranties`
- `Account liability`

## Eligibility

Recommended draft:

> You may use Clarity only if you can legally agree to these Terms and use the service under the laws that apply to you. If you use Clarity on behalf of another person or organization, you are responsible for having permission to do so.

Legal review note:

- Confirm whether the public Terms need a minimum age requirement before launch.
- Confirm whether Clarity is limited to the United States at launch.
- Confirm whether business/entity use is allowed or whether Clarity is personal-use only.

## Accurate Account Information

Recommended draft:

> You agree to provide accurate account information and keep it reasonably up to date. Clarity relies on the information you provide or connect to operate the product, including account access, support, privacy requests, and security notices.

Plain-language examples:

- Use an email address you control.
- Keep contact details current if they are used for account or privacy requests.
- Do not impersonate another person.
- Do not create accounts using information you are not authorized to use.

## Account Security

Recommended draft:

> You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and devices secure. Please tell us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission or if you notice suspicious activity.

Required support path:

> For account help, contact us at `[support/contact email]` or use the support path linked from the public site.

Security boundaries:

- Do not ask users to send passwords, bank credentials, full card numbers, Social Security numbers, or account numbers through support email.
- Do not promise account recovery outcomes that are not operationally guaranteed.
- Do not promise immediate response times unless support operations can meet them.

## User Responsibility For Connected Data

Recommended draft:

> If you connect accounts, upload information, or provide content to Clarity, you are responsible for having the right to do so. You should only connect accounts or provide information that belongs to you or that you are authorized to use with Clarity.

This supports Plaid readiness by making consent and authorization clear.

## Account Issues And Support

The Terms should identify how users get help for:

- Account access issues.
- Suspected unauthorized access.
- Account deletion requests.
- Privacy questions.
- Linked account disconnection questions.
- Billing questions if billing is added later.

Recommended draft:

> If you have trouble accessing your account, need help with a connected account, or want to make a privacy or deletion request, use the support or contact links on the public site.

Required links:

- `/contact`
- `/data-deletion`
- `/privacy`

## Prohibited Account Behavior

This section should briefly state that users may not:

- Share an account in a way that bypasses access controls.
- Use another person's account without permission.
- Attempt to access another user's data.
- Create accounts for fraud, abuse, or unauthorized testing.
- Misrepresent identity, affiliation, or authority.

Detailed misuse rules belong in Phase 6 Acceptable Use.

## Wording Rules

Use:

- `accurate account information`
- `keep your account secure`
- `tell us promptly`
- `authorized to connect or provide`
- `support path`

Avoid:

- `we guarantee account recovery`
- `we are liable for unauthorized access`
- `you waive all rights`
- `we respond immediately`
- Legalistic boilerplate that normal users will not understand

## Launch Review Questions

Resolve before publishing final Terms:

- What is the final support/contact email?
- Is Clarity available only in the United States at launch?
- What minimum age should apply?
- Is Clarity personal-use only?
- Is any billing/subscription flow planned for launch?
- What is the operational path for account recovery and deletion?

## Acceptance Checklist

- States users must provide accurate information.
- States users are responsible for account and device security.
- Provides a support path for account issues.
- Explains users should connect or provide only information they are authorized to use.
- Avoids unsupported recovery, response-time, or liability promises.
- Keeps detailed misuse rules deferred to the Acceptable Use phase.
