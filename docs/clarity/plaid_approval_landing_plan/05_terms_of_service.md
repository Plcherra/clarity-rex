# File 05 - Terms Of Service

Goal: create Terms that define acceptable use, product boundaries, disclaimers, and user responsibilities for Clarity.

## Phase 1 - Terms Scope

Goal: define what the Terms cover.

Files to modify/create:
- `/terms` page content

Acceptance Criteria:
- Covers Clarity mobile app, public site, and Rex assistant.
- Includes effective/last-updated date.
- Identifies operator/contact.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: missing entity details.
- Mitigation: flag legal review before publishing.

Effort: Small.

## Phase 2 - Eligibility And Accounts

Goal: explain who can use Clarity and account responsibilities.

Files to modify/create:
- Eligibility/account section

Acceptance Criteria:
- States users must provide accurate information.
- Users are responsible for account security.
- Support path exists for account issues.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: overcomplicated legal terms.
- Mitigation: keep clear and concise.

Effort: Small.

## Phase 3 - Financial Advice Boundary

Goal: avoid implying regulated advice.

Files to modify/create:
- Disclaimers section

Acceptance Criteria:
- States Clarity provides organization, analysis, and educational/coaching support.
- States Clarity is not a bank, broker, tax advisor, accountant, or financial advisor.
- Encourages professional advice for important decisions.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: copy undermines user confidence.
- Mitigation: phrase boundaries calmly and professionally.

Effort: Medium.

## Phase 4 - AI Assistant Disclaimer

Goal: set expectations for Rex.

Files to modify/create:
- Rex/AI section

Acceptance Criteria:
- States AI responses may be incomplete or inaccurate.
- Users should review important outputs.
- Rex does not make decisions or transactions for users.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: AI disclaimer sounds scary.
- Mitigation: explain as normal review expectation.

Effort: Small.

## Phase 5 - Plaid Connection Terms

Goal: explain user responsibility for connecting accounts.

Files to modify/create:
- Connected accounts section

Acceptance Criteria:
- States users authorize account connections.
- States availability depends on financial institutions and Plaid.
- States users can disconnect accounts.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: seeming to promise continuous bank connectivity.
- Mitigation: note third-party availability limitations.

Effort: Small.

## Phase 6 - Acceptable Use

Goal: define prohibited behavior.

Files to modify/create:
- Acceptable use section

Acceptance Criteria:
- Prohibits misuse, unauthorized access, reverse engineering, abuse, illegal activity, and uploading harmful content.
- Includes AI misuse boundaries.
- Keeps language user-readable.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: too broad or vague.
- Mitigation: use standard examples without excessive detail.

Effort: Small.

## Phase 7 - Limitation And Changes

Goal: cover service changes, availability, and liability boundaries.

Files to modify/create:
- Availability/liability section

Acceptance Criteria:
- States service may change or be interrupted.
- Includes reasonable limitation of liability draft.
- Includes terms update process.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: legal clauses need jurisdiction-specific review.
- Mitigation: mark for attorney review.

Effort: Medium.

## Phase 8 - Terms Review Gate

Goal: prepare Terms for launch review.

Files to modify/create:
- Terms review notes

Acceptance Criteria:
- No placeholders remain.
- Terms align with landing page claims.
- Legal review TODO is explicit before production launch.

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: Terms contradict Privacy.
- Mitigation: review both together.

Effort: Small.
